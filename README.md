Hermoso AI is an AI marketing agent with an AI ad generator built in. It creates video and image ads, posts them, and runs campaigns from the web or via AI assistants. The platform offers tools for competitor ad research, multi‑channel marketing, and integration with Google Analytics 4, enabling users to automate end‑to‑end marketing operations.

**Areas:** Company · AI · Marketing · Advertising · Automation

## How this profile is made
